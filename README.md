# YojanaSetu

YojanaSetu is a scheme eligibility navigator that helps people discover Indian government welfare schemes that may be relevant to them. It aims to make government scheme information easier to understand by helping users explore eligibility requirements, learn about available benefits, identify required documents, and find official application information.

The platform uses a questionnaire to collect relevant user details and helps users explore schemes based on their responses. It aims to make eligibility requirements more transparent by explaining which conditions appear to match, which do not, and where more information is needed. YojanaSetu also includes multilingual support to make scheme information more accessible to people from different language backgrounds.

The project currently focuses on five government schemes across three categories: education, women, and Below Poverty Line (BPL) households. Scheme information should be checked against official government sources, including the [myScheme portal](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/).

## Features

- **Scheme Discovery:** Helps users explore government welfare schemes relevant to their profiles.
- **Eligibility Checker:** Compares user-provided information with available scheme eligibility conditions.
- **Explainable Results:** Helps users understand which eligibility conditions match, do not match, or require additional information.
- **Benefits Information:** Presents information about the benefits offered by schemes.
- **Document Readiness Checker:** Helps users identify documents that may be required when applying.
- **Personalized Action Plan:** Guides users towards the next steps for exploring and applying for schemes.
- **Scheme Comparison:** Helps users compare relevant schemes and their requirements.
- **Multilingual Support:** Makes the platform accessible to users who prefer different languages.

## Scheme Data Sources

YojanaSetu uses government scheme information to help users understand eligibility requirements and application procedures.

- [myScheme: Government Scheme Discovery Portal](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/)

Information may also be collected from relevant official ministry and government department websites. Each scheme's data should include its official source link and the date on which the information was last verified.

## Accuracy and Limitations

YojanaSetu is a hackathon prototype intended to help users explore government welfare schemes. Its results are informational and do not guarantee eligibility, benefit approval, or successful application.

Eligibility results depend on the information provided by users and the accuracy and completeness of the available scheme data. Missing information should be identified rather than treated as proof of eligibility or ineligibility. Users should consult the relevant official government sources before applying.
