ROADMAP (For Hacktober fest)

01 Welcome page
Explain what YojanaSetu does and let users start.

02 User questionnaire
Collect only the details needed to evaluate schemes.

03 Eligibility analysis
Compare the user's answers with each scheme's rules.

04 Personalised scheme results
Show potentially eligible, not eligible, and needs-more-information results.

05 Why am I eligible or not?
Explain each condition with PASS, FAIL, or UNKNOWN and show its source.

06 Understand the benefits
Explain benefits, limits, frequency, and important restrictions.

07 Document checklist
Show required documents and let users mark what they already have.

08 Get missing documents
Explain where and how to obtain each missing document, step by step.

09 Apply for the scheme
Provide official application links, process instructions, and a final checklist.
